FAQ

What is a Grok Bot template?

A ready-made setup for a Grok Bot: its skills, instructions and routines. Adding a template gives you your own copy of that bot, which you can then customise.

Are these templates free?

Yes. Every template and every pack here is free to add. Running a bot uses your normal Grok Bot plan.

Do I have to pay or support to use a template or pack?

No. All templates and packs are free. Support is optional, and nothing here depends on it: every template link opens directly, with no payment step in between. If you want to give back, the pay-what-you-want support link is at the bottom of the page.

How do I use a template?

Tap "Use this template". It opens the template on x.ai, where you add it to your Grok Bot and answer a few setup questions. The bot then starts working for you.

Will a bot post, pay or submit things without asking me?

These templates are written to ask for your yes before anything goes public, costs money or gets submitted. Each description says what it does on its own.

Do the templates contain anyone's personal data?

No. Each template is a generic copy with no logins, accounts or personal details. You connect your own accounts after you add it.

How do I find the right templates for my business?

Use "Find your bundle": describe what you want done and what kind of business you run, or tap a quick pick such as Lawyer, Creator or Startup. You get a matched bundle with a reason for each pick, and a link you can share.

Who makes these templates?
